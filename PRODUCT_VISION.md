# Learnova - Product Vision

# Vision

Build the world's most intelligent AI-powered learning platform that provides every student with a personalized learning experience.

Learnova will begin by focusing on **Chemistry**, one of the most challenging STEM subjects for students, and gradually expand into other subjects such as Mathematics, Physics, Biology, English, Computer Science, and beyond.

Instead of replacing teachers, Learnova acts as a personal AI tutor that understands every student's strengths, weaknesses, learning style, and pace. The platform continuously adapts learning paths to maximize understanding and long-term retention.

Our long-term vision is to become the **AI Learning Companion** that students use every day throughout their educational journey.

---

# Problem

Traditional education faces several major challenges.

## One-size-fits-all Learning

Every student learns differently, but classrooms typically deliver the same content at the same pace for everyone.

## Lack of Personalized Guidance

Students often struggle to identify:

- What they already understand
- What concepts they have forgotten
- Which topics they should learn next
- Why they keep making similar mistakes

## Passive Learning

Most online learning platforms only deliver videos or documents. Students consume content without active feedback or personalized coaching.

## Difficulty Connecting Concepts

Subjects like Chemistry contain many interconnected concepts. Students often memorize formulas without understanding the relationships between ideas.

## Limited Progress Visibility

Students and parents rarely have a clear picture of:

- Current skill level
- Knowledge gaps
- Learning trends
- Mastery over time

---

# Solution

Learnova combines Artificial Intelligence, Knowledge Graphs, and Adaptive Learning to create a truly personalized educational experience.

## AI Tutor

An intelligent tutor capable of:

- Answering questions naturally
- Explaining concepts at different difficulty levels
- Providing hints instead of direct answers
- Guiding students through problem-solving step by step
- Generating examples based on student ability
- Remembering previous learning history

---

## Knowledge Graph (KG)

Every concept is represented as part of a connected knowledge graph.

Benefits include:

- Understanding prerequisite relationships
- Identifying missing foundational knowledge
- Visualizing learning paths
- Suggesting prerequisite topics before advanced concepts
- Detecting hidden knowledge gaps

Example:

```
Atoms
    ↓
Molecules
    ↓
Chemical Bonds
    ↓
Chemical Reactions
    ↓
Stoichiometry
```

Instead of treating each lesson independently, Learnova understands how all concepts are connected.

---

## Recommendation Engine

The recommendation engine continuously analyzes student behavior to recommend:

- What to study next
- Which concepts need review
- Appropriate practice questions
- Revision schedules
- Personalized learning paths

Recommendations are generated using:

- Skill mastery
- Knowledge graph relationships
- Practice history
- Learning pace
- Error patterns
- Student goals

# MVP (Minimum Viable Product)

The first release focuses on validating the core learning experience.

## AI Chat Tutor

Students can:

- Ask Chemistry questions
- Receive detailed explanations
- Request simpler or more advanced explanations
- Generate examples
- Continue conversations with context

---

## Practice System

Interactive exercises including:

- Multiple choice
- Fill in the blank
- Short answer
- Step-by-step reasoning
- AI-generated practice questions

Immediate feedback is provided after each attempt.

---

## Skill Tracking

Track learning progress at the concept level.

Features include:

- Mastered skills
- Skills in progress
- Weak areas
- Learning streaks
- Accuracy statistics
- Practice history
- Estimated mastery percentage

Students can clearly see what they have learned and what still needs improvement.

# Target Users

## Primary

- Secondary school students

## Secondary

- Teachers

---

# Success Metrics

The MVP will be considered successful if it achieves:

- High Daily Active Users (DAU)
- Strong student retention
- Increased practice completion rate
- Improved concept mastery over time
- Positive user satisfaction with the AI Tutor
- Measurable reduction in repeated mistakes

---

# Long-Term Roadmap

After validating the Chemistry MVP, Learnova will expand by:

- Supporting additional STEM subjects
- Building cross-subject Knowledge Graphs
- Introducing adaptive learning paths
- Adding gamification and achievements
- Enabling collaborative classroom features
- Supporting multilingual learning
- Providing analytics dashboards for teachers and parents
- Integrating voice interaction, image recognition, and interactive simulations

Ultimately, Learnova aims to become a lifelong AI learning companion that helps every learner achieve their full potential through personalized, data-driven education.
